Privacy Policy

How customer details are handled.

This page explains how Mico Industrial Press plans to use contact details, quote information, and optional uploaded files submitted through the website.

Request Quote Chat on WhatsApp

Information We Collect

When customers use the quote or contact features, the website may collect name, phone number, email address, organisation name, service requested, quantity, job details, deadline, pickup or delivery preference, and optional file uploads.

How The Information Is Used

Customer information is used to understand the print request, prepare a useful quote, contact the customer about the job, and keep communication clear during the quote process.

Quote Requests And Emails

In the backend phase, quote requests will be sent to the business email address and a confirmation email may be sent to the customer. The frontend currently shows the form UI only.

Optional Uploads

The quote form includes upload structure for artwork or samples. When backend upload handling is added, accepted file types and maximum file sizes will be enforced for safety.

Information Sharing

Customer details should only be used by Mico Industrial Press for responding to customer requests. They should not be sold or shared for unrelated marketing.

Security

The production backend should use validation, upload limits, spam protection, environment variables, and secure email handling. Sensitive credentials must never be placed in frontend files.
